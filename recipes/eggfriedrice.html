Egg Fried Rice

Home

Description

Fluffy,aromatic,easy to make egg fried rice is satisfying lunch or dinner recipe after a hard day of work.

Best thing is you can tweak the recipe however you please,change the veggies or sauce or meat, it'll still end up as satisfying.

So,without furthur debating, let's get into it!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice (well-cooled or day-old)
  • 2 to 3 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, slit (optional)
  • 3 to 4 stalks spring onions (greens & whites separated, chopped)
  • 1/2 cup carrots, finely chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 cup capsicum / bell peppers, diced (optional)
  • 1/2 cup cabbage, shredded (optional)
  • 1 to 1 & 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce (or tamari)
  • 1 & 1/2 teaspoons rice vinegar (optional)
  • 1 to 1 & 1/2 tablespoons Schezwan sauce or hot sauce (optional)
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Prep Aromatics: Heat oil in a wok or pan on high flame, then sauté the chopped garlic, green chili, and spring onion whites for 1 minute.
  2. Stir-Fry Veggies: Add the chopped carrots, bell peppers, and cabbage; stir-fry on high heat for 2 minutes until tender-粒crisp.
  3. Scramble Eggs: Push the vegetables to the side, lower the heat, pour in the beaten eggs, and scramble until soft-set with a pinch of salt.
  4. Add Sauces: Turn heat back up and pour soy sauce, rice vinegar, and optional hot sauce along the sides of the hot pan to sizzle and release flavor.
  5. Combine & Toss: Add the cooled rice, ground black pepper, remaining salt, and spring onion greens.
  6. Finish & Serve: Stir-fry everything on high heat for 2 to 3 minutes until well combined, garnish with extra green onions, and serve hot.
