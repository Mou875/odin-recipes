Chocochip Cookie

Home

Description

One of the most popular sweet treat and definitely the most favourite of all cookies, a chewy, soft, melts in your mouth chocochip chip cookie can never not make your day.

Whether you have been baking for years, or you have never baked, this recipe deserves a try.

Let's go!

Ingredients

  • 2 and 1/4 cups (281g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup (170g/12 Tbsp) unsalted butter, melted & cooled for 5 minutes
  • 3/4 cup (150g) packed light or dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg + 1 egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cups (225g) semi-sweet chocolate chips or chocolate chunks

Steps

  1. Mix Dry Ingredients: Whisk the flour, baking soda, cornstarch, and salt in a large bowl and set aside.
  2. Combine Wet Ingredients: Whisk the melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar, then mix in the egg, egg yolk, and vanilla extract.
  3. Make Dough & Fold Chips: Stir the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, then fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Chill: Cover tightly and refrigerate the dough for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
  5. Prep & Shape: Preheat oven to 325°F (163°C) with lined baking sheets; scoop dough into tall, cylinder-like balls and place 3 inches apart.
  6. Bake & Cool: Bake for 13–14 minutes until edges are lightly browned, let set on the sheet for 10 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack.
  7. Store: Keep in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 week.
